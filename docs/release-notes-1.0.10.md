# Mogbot 1.0.10

## What's new in 1.0.10

- **Dictation that gets your words right** - When you stop talking, your computer writes what you said out again with Whisper, a speech model that runs on your computer, then fixes the names of your agents and projects, using the conversation you're in. Undo puts back what it first heard. Enter sends straight from dictation and Esc cancels. The speech model is a one-time 574 MB download.
- **Dictation on Windows and Linux** - The microphone works there too, with the same speech model.
- **Phone pictures show** - Result cards with several pictures no longer appear as blank space on the phone.
- **Needs your OK opens the card** - Tapping a "Needs your OK" line goes straight to that card, and your answer stays in the conversation that asked.
- **Fixes from the beta** - OpenCode no longer offers accounts it can't use, a typing mistake in a name is said plainly instead of reported as an error, message menus stay inside the chat, dictation that's switched off in macOS says where to turn it on, usage no longer steps backwards, the last words of a dictation aren't dropped, and agents talk to you as "you".
- **Dictation with Bluetooth headphones** - Dictation uses the built-in mic when your headphones would switch to call quality, and keeps listening when the audio route changes.
