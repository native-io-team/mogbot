# Mogbot 1.0.2

## What's new in 1.0.2

- **Set up in a conversation** - after you pick an engine, the Chief of Staff takes it from there in its chat: it asks whether you already have agents somewhere else (Grok, ChatGPT, Claude…) and walks you through bringing them over one step at a time, ticking each step off by itself
- **A clearer installer** - the download opens on a window that shows what to do: drag Mogbot into Applications
- **Your phone stays connected** - while a phone is paired your Mac stays awake for it, and after the Mac wakes it reconnects at once
- **Agents check the team first** - before asking you or redoing work, an agent looks at what its teammates already did
- **Reply while a card waits** - typing a message while an agent waits on your answer sends it straight away instead of queuing it
- **Keep changes during a tidy-up** - Keep no longer waits behind an agent tidying its memory
- **Polish** - a clearer queue hint under the message box, and steadier checks across the app
