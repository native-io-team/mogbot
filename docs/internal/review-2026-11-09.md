# Adversarial review, 2026-11-09

A demanding new user's and a design lead's read of the app on `mogbot/foundation` (2d4f2aa5),
measured against Linear, Raycast, Arc and Things, and against Reason Machines, OpenBot and Grok Bot
(`competitors-2026-11.md`), the brand note (`brand-direction.md`) and `MISSION.md`.

**How it was looked at.** Everything below was seen, not inferred:

- Desk: `pnpm harness` + `node scripts/harness-capture.mjs`, light (304 scenarios, every
  `agents-*`, `onboarding-*`, `settings-agents-*`, `dashboard-*`, `project-tools*`) and dark (the
  onboarding, new-agent, result, login, dashboard and settings sets). Ids below are the harness
  scenario ids: open `harness.html?scenario=<id>` to see each one.
- Phone: `cd mobile && pnpm qa:web` (demo, pairing, chats, cards, settings; light and dark).
- Site: https://mogbot.app at 1440 and 390 wide, scrolled through with a real clock.

**Not reported, on purpose:** avatars (being replaced by agent keys, `agent-keys.md`) and the
Agents/Projects switch (being fixed elsewhere).

**Not checked:** the real Tauri build in WKWebView (the harness is Chrome), keyboard walk-throughs
beyond the harness's keyboard scenarios, and VoiceOver. The `--commands` palette sweep ran on its
own (130 captures, none crashed, unsettled or unmocked) but each capture was not looked at.

Ranked worst first. Each item: where, what's wrong, why it matters, the fix.

---

## 1. Four vocabularies for one idea: how much an agent does on its own

**Where.** `onboarding-independence`; `settings-agents-page` ("What agents can do", "What your
agents do on their own", "Independence", "Admins"); `agents-new`, `agents-permissions` (Edit
agent); `agents-model-menu` ("What it does on its own…"). Code: `src-tauri/src/agents/policy.rs:218`
(presets), `src/lib/agents.ts:1079` (`engineProtections`), `src/components/agents/IndependenceSlider.tsx`,
`src/components/agents/AgentActions.tsx`.

**What's wrong.** The same question is answered by:
1. the four-stop slider (Careful / Steady / Bold / Unleashed), titled "How much it does on its own";
2. a second, three-step preset scale on the same settings page, "What agents can do":
   *I trust my agents* (= Bold), *Balanced* (= Steady), *Locked down* (= Careful), with
   "Reset everyone to Balanced";
3. an engine paragraph in New/Edit agent: "It can edit your projects and use this app's tools on
   its own. Anything else asks you first", shown directly above a level picker whose Steady line
   says "asks before running changes". A reader sees two contradictory answers in one dialog;
4. menu and page titles that drift: "What it does on its own…", "How much it does on its own",
   "What your agents do on their own", "Independence", "level".

And the site's Steady ("They finish the job and ask only when a choice is really yours") is not
the app's Steady ("Reads and edits; asks before running changes or anything leaving this Mac").

**Why it matters.** This is the product's trust contract. Grok Bot has one card with Allow once /
Always allow / Deny and one rules list. We have two scales whose names don't line up, and a
paragraph that contradicts the slider. "One word per idea" fails at its most important idea.

**Fix.** One scale, one name. Keep the four stops; fold the presets into it (the preset is just the
project/app/browser reach that each stop implies) or rename them to the stops they set ("Set
everyone to Steady"). Call the concept **Independence** everywhere (menu item "Independence…",
page section "Independence", the editor row "Independence"). Move `engineProtections` out of the
editor's main column into the engine's own detail (Advanced), worded as what the *engine* enforces
under the level ("Claude Code enforces: file edits only in its project…"). Make the site use the
app's stop lines verbatim (`packages/companion-protocol/src/independence.ts` is the source).

## 2. Two "Accounts" that mean different things, and the same account under three names

**Where.** `dashboard` (an "Accounts" block: "Logins for ● Default — each workspace signs in
separately", ENGINES / SERVICES), `settings-agents-accounts` (an ordered list, "This Mac", "Work",
"Personal"), `onboarding-accounts` ("sam@example.com · this Mac's own sign-in"), the sidebar foot
("sam@example.com"), `settings-agents-usage` ("Claude · This Mac", "Claude Code · Work").
Code: `src/components/dashboard/AgentsPanel.tsx:524`, `src/components/setup/flow/AccountsStep.tsx:145`.

**What's wrong.** "Accounts" on Projects is installed CLIs + GitHub/Vercel per workspace; "Accounts"
in Settings is the usage-failover order. The first account is "sam@example.com" in onboarding and
the sidebar, "This Mac" in Settings → Accounts (the onboarding scenario's own note says *never "This
Mac" as a name*), and "Claude · This Mac" in Usage, where the next line says "Claude Code · Work".

**Why it matters.** Account stacking is the site's lead promise ("Add every account you have").
A user who adds a second account in onboarding and opens Settings will not recognise their own
first one.

**Fix.** Rename the dashboard block **Tools and services** (it is CLIs and hosts, not accounts).
Name an account by its email everywhere (fall back to "This Mac's sign-in" only when the engine
won't say), and say the engine once as "Claude Code" (never bare "Claude").

## 3. The site sells things the app doesn't do

**Where.** Site hero and chapters vs `agents-new-advanced`, `settings-agents-usage`.

**What's wrong.** The site: "Every Claude, ChatGPT and open model you have", an agent editor with
**Claude Code | Codex | OpenCode** and "Runs on this computer through Ollama. No usage limit",
Download buttons for Windows and Linux, and "One for invoices… through your Chrome". The app: Runs
on is Claude Code | Codex only; Settings says of Ollama and Opencode "Agents don't run on it";
the dashboard lists Opencode as "Projects only". `brand.json` / CLAUDE.md describe a Mac app.

**Why it matters.** First-run trust. The first thing a site visitor tries (an OpenCode agent) isn't
there.

**Fix.** Either ship OpenCode as an agent engine or take it, the Ollama row and Windows/Linux off
the site until they exist. Mark anything coming as "Soon", as the phone button already does.

## 4. A login card whose button did something other than what it said (fixed), and still too much text

**Where.** `agents-login-failed`, `agents-login-card` (desk); `login-choose`, `login-other-ways` (phone).
`src/components/agents/AgentLoginCard.tsx:117-142`.

**What was wrong.** After the claude.ai route failed, the card's lead button (Use my browser) was
relabelled **Try again** while still starting the *browser* route, and the error line beside it
said "Choose Use my browser instead", a button that no longer existed. The phone already got this
right (`mobile/src/components/LoginCard.tsx:484`). **Fixed here** (`retriesPrimary` in
`src/lib/agentLogins.ts`, test in `AgentLoginCard.test.tsx`).

**Still wrong.** The card is five paragraphs at 11–12px (asked for, what it can do, how the browser
route works, the claude.ai route, its own fine print), and the buttons are **Not now / Not needed /
Use my browser**. "Not now" and "Not needed" are near-synonyms side by side; the difference (ask
again later vs never ask the team again) lives in a hover title.

**Fix.** Lead line + abilities + one line for the chosen route; the other route behind "Other ways
in". Rename **Not needed** to **Don't ask again** (it is exactly that), keep Not now.

## 5. One request, five signals

**Where.** `agents-team`, `agents-approval`.

**What's wrong.** A single pending approval from Lead Gen shows: an amber "Needs your OK · 1" row
under the Chief, an amber "Needs your OK" pill in Lead Gen's row, an amber dot at the row's right,
a "Lead Gen needs your OK · 1 request · Review" bar above the Chief's composer, and an entry in
What's in flight. The right-edge dot column also means three different things: amber = needs you,
black (the Chief's accent) = unread, red = failed.

**Why it matters.** Things and Linear show one count in one place. Five echoes of one ask make the
sidebar shout, and the dot column can't be read without a legend.

**Fix.** Keep the pill in the row and the bar in the open chat; drop the trailing amber/red dot
when the row already carries a pill (the dot becomes unread-only), and drop the "Needs your OK · N"
sidebar row (the bell already counts). `src/components/agents/AgentsSidebar.tsx:103,147,323`.

## 6. The agent "…" menu is 25 items and runs off the window

**Where.** `agents-model-menu`. `src/components/agents/AgentActions.tsx`, `AgentModelMenu.tsx`.

**What's wrong.** Pin, Mark as unread, Mute, Files, Schedule, Edit, What it does on its own, Take
over, Start fresh, Hide, Delete, then Model (4), Effort (4), Routines model (…), in one list that
is cut off at the bottom of a 900px window ("Automatic" half visible).

**Why it matters.** Raycast and Linear cap action menus at about ten and push settings into the
editor. Delete sits mid-list between Hide and a model picker.

**Fix.** Menu = actions only (≤ 10, Delete last after a divider). Model, effort and routines model
become one "Model…" submenu, or live only in Edit agent → Advanced and the header's model chip.

## 7. New agent offers four ways to start at once

**Where.** `agents-new`, `agents-new-advanced`, `agents-describe-drafted` (light and dark).
`src/components/agents/AgentEditorModal.tsx`, `AgentDescribe.tsx:75`.

**What's wrong.** Describe + Draft it, then "Or start from Blank | Research | Inbox | Weekly
Review", then the full manual form, then "Not sure what to write? Ask your Chief of Staff", then
Advanced. The describe box's placeholder is a full, realistic three-line brief at the same size as
real text, so the empty box reads as filled while Draft it looks disabled (it is, correctly). In
Advanced the footer cuts "Apps this agent can use" in half.

**Why it matters.** OpenBot's flow is the competitor benchmark: describe, review, save. Ours
front-loads every path, and the most important one looks broken.

**Fix.** Open on the describe box alone with a short placeholder ("What should it do? e.g. 'Brief
me every weekday at 8'"), Draft it as the strong button, and "Fill it in myself" / templates as
quiet links that reveal the form. Keep the manual form exactly as is once revealed.

## 8. Settings → Agents is one 10,800px scroll inside a 500px modal

**Where.** `settings-agents-page` (the capture is 964 × 10,852 at 1x), `settings-agents`.

**What's wrong.** Every group (On this Mac, Admins, What agents can do, Independence, Engines and
models, Usage, Accounts, Notifications, Away from your desk, Slack, Chrome…) is stacked in one
modal column with five tabs above it.

**Why it matters.** Linear, Raycast and Arc give settings a full window with a section list. A
twelve-screen scroll defeats "everything changeable from chat" being backed by a findable page.

**Fix.** Settings as a full-window view with a left section list (each current group a section),
or at least split Agents into Agents / Usage and accounts / Phone and away / Integrations tabs.

## 9. Sidebar rows truncate the role to a few letters

**Where.** `agents-team` and every agents scenario: "Chief of Staff Runs t…", "Social Posts
Drafts p…", "Native App The Expo …". `src/components/agents/AgentsSidebar.tsx:436`.

**What's wrong.** The role shares the name's line and gives way first, so it shows three to six
letters plus an ellipsis, followed by pin/mute icons. The role is already in the chat header.

**Why it matters.** It is pure noise on the most-looked-at surface, and the brand note's goal was
name + one crisp status line (Reason Machines' row).

**Fix.** Drop the role from the row (keep it in the header and as the row's `title`), or hide it
with a container query below ~12 visible characters.

## 10. "Out of usage" is said five ways

**Where.** `agents-quota-limited`, `agents-team-failures`, `settings-agents-accounts`, the site.
`src/lib/agents.ts:2237`, `src-tauri/src/agents/failure.rs:77,123`, `src/lib/agentAccounts.ts`.

**What's wrong.** "Out of usage" (pill) + "resets 6:45 PM"; "Out of usage until 7:46 PM";
"Claude Code's usage limit is reached. It resets at 6:45 PM."; "Usage limit · resets …"; the site's
"Out until 5:57 PM".

**Fix.** One phrase: **Out of usage · back at 6:45 PM** for pills and rows, and the chat line
"Claude Code is out of usage until 6:45 PM." Make the site match.

## 11. Result cards lead with caveats and shrink the work

**Where.** `agents-result-v1`, `agents-result-v2`, `agents-result-not-verified`.
`src/components/agents/AgentResult.tsx:167`.

**What's wrong.** Order is summary → "Not verified" box → files → "It says it checked". What wasn't
checked and what was are split by the file list, and the whole card is set at 11–12px beside a
chat at 14–15px. A link whose title is its own URL repeats the host as its subtitle
("www.linkedin.com/company/…" / "www.linkedin.com").

**Why it matters.** The card is the deliverable. Honesty is right; burying the work under it and
typesetting it smaller than chatter is not.

**Fix.** Summary → files → one Checks block (✓ checked, ? not verified, side by side) → Ask for
changes. Card body at the chat's body size. Drop the subtitle when it equals the title's host.

## 12. Schedule and When… popovers are overloaded and clip at both ends

**Where.** `agents-schedule-edit`, `agents-when-routines`. `src/components/agents/AgentSchedulePopover.tsx:336`,
`AgentWhenRoutine.tsx:51-72`.

**What's wrong.** Existing routines, a new-routine form, a cost note and a stay-awake note share
one popover that is cut off at the top ("Past runs · 1" half visible) and the bottom (the
stay-awake sentence ends mid-line) with no fade or scroll cue. Every routine row has a red
**Delete** at the same weight as Edit. The When… choices are first person ("mentions me") and the
readback second person ("mentions you").

**Fix.** Split into the list (popover) and the editor (its own sheet/dialog). Delete into a row
menu. One person throughout: the readback follows the choice ("When someone mentions me on
GitHub, Lead Gen starts on it"). Pin the stay-awake note as a one-line footer.

## 13. Off switches look on

**Where.** `settings-agents`, `settings-agents-admins` (light): an off switch is a dark grey knob
on a pale track; on is a black track with a white knob.

**Why it matters.** Accessibility: state is carried by a subtle knob position and two greys that
both read as "filled". macOS and Linear use a white knob on a grey track for off.

**Fix.** Off knob = surface white (`--surface-raised`) with the existing ring; track
`--border-default`. Check AA for the track against the card in both themes.

## 14. Projects looks like a different, older product

**Where.** `dashboard`, `dashboard-many`, `workspace-project-agents`.

**What's wrong.** 24 identical grey "No preview" blocks; uppercase tracked micro-labels (PINNED,
ACTIVE, AGENTS, SESSIONS, TERMINALS, DEV SERVER: `src/styles/features/workspace/sidebar.css:441`,
`dock.css:261,698`) where the brand note moved Agents to sentence-case 13px titles; four unlabelled
icons top left; a project's "3 changes" in amber, the needs-you colour (**fixed here**: now
`--text-secondary`).

**Fix.** A real placeholder per project (framework mark or first letter on the key material, or the
last screenshot); sentence-case section titles to match Agents; labels or tooltips on the icon row.

## 15. Onboarding: warnings before anything went wrong, and two progress systems

**Where.** `onboarding-tailscale-mac-missing`, `onboarding-tailscale-phone-none`,
`onboarding-independence`, `onboarding-engine-pick`, `onboarding-engine-install`.

**What's wrong.** Arriving on a step shows an amber triangle "Not done yet." before the user has
tried anything. The independence step has Continue *and* Skip, which do the same thing when the
slider is untouched. Engine cards are 11px text in a mostly empty 1440px canvas, with a tiny
compact "Install & sign in →" button. Progress is shown twice: a 5-segment bar and "Claude Code ·
1 of 2" / "Your phone · 1 of 4".

**Fix.** Neutral "Not done yet" (no triangle, muted) until the first Check again or 20 s; drop
Skip on independence (Continue keeps the default); make the engine card itself the button at
`--font-size-md`; fold the sub-count into the bar's label ("Your AI · 1 of 2") and drop the other.

## 16. Header controls without words

**Where.** Chat header in every agents scenario. `src/components/agents/AgentUsageRing.tsx:58-72`.

**What's wrong.** With no usage reading the usage control renders as an empty dashed circle (reads
as a spinner); routines use the history icon (clock with a back-arrow), which everyone reads as
"history". Only "3 scheduled" has words, and only when there are routines.

**Fix.** Hide the ring until there is a reading (or show "Usage" text); use a calendar/clock icon
for routines and keep its count.

## 17. Per-agent accent collides with meaning

**Where.** `agents-quota-limited`, `agents-usage-limit` ("Retry at 6:45 PM" in Finance's
terracotta reads as an error), `agents-permissions` (green Save), `agents-schedule-edit` (orange
Save), `agents-when-routines` (teal Add).

**Why it matters.** The rule "accent = primary action, never status" holds in code, but an agent
whose colour is red or green makes primary actions look like danger or success, and every dialog's
main button is a different hue.

**Fix.** Text-link actions on status lines neutral (`--text-primary`, underline on hover); keep
the accent for the one primary button per surface, and clamp per-agent accents away from the
error/success hues (or use the neutral keycap for primary inside an agent's own dialogs).

## 18. The Chief's greeting is laid out unlike every other message

**Where.** `onboarding-end-to-agents`, `agents-no-engine`.

**What's wrong.** It is a centred paragraph mid-screen with centred starter chips that all end in
"…" (intentional: they fill the composer, but they read as truncated).

**Fix.** Left-align it as the Chief's first message; starters as full short phrases ("Brief me
every morning") with the composer fill as the click result, no ellipsis.
