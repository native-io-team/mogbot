# Mogbot 1.0.6

## What's new in 1.0.6

- **Your phone, without Tailscale** - Sign in on your Mac (Google or email) and your phone reaches it from anywhere through an end-to-end encrypted relay that can't read your messages. Prefer not to sign in? Everything still works over your own Tailscale.
- **Notifications when the phone app is closed** - "An agent needs you" reaches your iPhone even when the app isn't open. On by default; switch it off in the Phone panel.
- **Delete my account** - Settings → Account removes your email and account for good. Nothing on your Mac is deleted.
- **Dictation that looks like typing** - Your words land where typed text goes, with Listening · Cancel on its own line.
- **Tell the Chief something's broken** - It writes it up, you OK the exact words, and it goes to us.
- **Error reports from your phone** - Phone crashes reach us through your Mac, with a switch in Settings. Reports keep only file names, never your folder names.
- **Faster reconnects** - Coming back to the phone app reconnects in seconds.
