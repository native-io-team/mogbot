A room is one thread for a job that needs several agents: a launch, a month end, a hire. Instead
of carrying answers from one chat to the next, you put the agents in one place and they talk to
each other.

## Making one

Click the people button beside **New agent**, or ask the Chief of Staff to make one. Say what
it's for ("Launch the new pricing page by Thursday"), pick two to six agents, and choose who
answers when you don't name anyone.

## Talking in it

- **Ask once, everyone hears it.** Your message goes to the agent you @mention, to all of them
  with @everyone, or to the room's lead when you name nobody.
- **They pass work to each other.** When one agent @mentions another in its reply, that agent
  picks it up. Up to eight replies follow one message of yours.
- A new room offers three first messages to start with ("Where does each of you stand?" and two
  more). Tap one to put it in the message box, then send it or change it first.

## What they know

Each agent brings what it already knows from its own chats with you, and its memory. What it
says or reads in the room goes back with it, so it's there the next time you talk to it alone.
Nothing from an agent's own chat is posted in the room unless the agent says it there.
