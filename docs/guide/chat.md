Every message is a bubble. Yours takes your agent's colour (with writing in near-black or near-white, whichever reads best on that colour) on the right; your agent's is a faint tint of the text colour on the left. Each has a small square corner at the bottom toward whoever spoke. Cards (results, approvals, tables and charts) keep their own frames and are not bubbles.

A reply appears all at once, not word by word: while your agent writes it, the one working line stays at the end of the chat, and when the reply is ready it takes that line's place. The same goes in a room.

## The header on your phone

The top of a chat on your phone shows only who you're talking to: the agent's key, its name on one line, a small ⌄, and **⋯** at the right. A small dot after the ⌄ says if something needs a look: amber when something waits on you (a request or a task), red when its last turn didn't finish, blue while it works, and no dot when it's idle or paused for the refill.

Tap the name to open a sheet with everything about the agent right now. It shows who it is and what it runs on, **Now** (what it's doing, waiting for your OK, why it didn't finish with **Try again**, or Idle), its top tasks with **All tasks**, **Needs your OK** with **Review** (which closes the sheet and takes you to the first card), its **Conversations** (the one you're in has a tick; tap another to switch, or **New conversation**), and **Edit agent** and **Instructions and memory** at the foot. A part with nothing to show is left out. Swipe down to close it. Conversations are now switched only here, not from ⋯.

## Reply to a message

Hover a message and one quiet **⋯** appears beside it, level with its first line (left of yours, right of the agent's); Tab reaches it too. Click it (or press Return) for the message's menu, beside it in words: **Copy**, **Reply**, and on your own messages **Edit and resend**, **Fork from here** and **Rewind to here**, then **Remember this**. While it's open the ⋯ is just brighter; Esc closes it. ⌘C still copies a focused message. With a message focused, press **R** (Esc drops it again); on your own messages the right-click menu has Reply first too. The message you're replying to shows as one outlined line at the top inside the message box, with a × to cancel. One reply at a time; replying to another replaces it. Your message then goes with a one-line pill above its bubble showing what it answers (a name first only when it quotes someone other than you and this agent), and your agent is told which message you meant. Click the pill to jump to the original and see it flash; if the original is no longer loaded the pill says "Message not available". The ⋯ isn't drawn on touch screens or in a window narrower than 480 pixels; the right-click menu and R still work there.

## When your agent is busy

## The message box

On the desk the box is a softly rounded rectangle whose corners match its buttons, and on the phone it is a round bar. On the desk it is one row: a **+** (attach files) on the left, your words, then the mic and Send on the right. Once your words wrap, or in a narrow box such as a project's Session panel, the words take the whole width and the +, the mic and Send sit in a row under them. The model isn't in the box: click the agent's name at the top, then **Next turn**. Send is your bubble's colour on the same chrome as the other buttons (a fine border and a soft shadow): a muted arrow while the box is empty, lit once there is something to send, and a stop square while your agent is working and the box is empty; Stop stops the turn. On the phone, change the model from **Model and controls** in the ⋯ menu.

### Pictures in the middle of what you say

Paste, drop or attach a picture (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP or SVG) and it goes into your words as **[Image 1]** where the caret is, the way a terminal does it; the next is **[Image 2]**, and you can add up to ten files. Each picture's chip above the box carries the same number. Delete the marker (Backspace takes all of it) and the picture goes with it; remove the chip and its marker goes too, and the others are numbered again so they stay 1, 2, 3. If you haven't clicked into the box yet, a dropped picture goes after your words. Once sent, each picture sits inside your message at the place you put it, and the agent is told which file is which image. Other files (a PDF, a spreadsheet) still sit above your message. This works in a chat, a room and Quick entry. On the phone your pictures still sit above the message for now.

Send something while it is working and it waits its turn. Under your message you see **Next**, and **Send now** if you'd rather it stop and read yours straight away. The agent's one working line sits below; that is all the chat says about it.

## A page above the chat

An agent can keep a page for a conversation, like a tracker or a status board. It only sits above the chat if you asked for that. Otherwise it is kept, and the **Page** button in the chat header pins it.

## Files it names

Every path in a reply shows as a small monospace chip. When it names a file in the agent's folder or one of your projects, click the chip to open the file in its usual app (⌘-click shows it in Finder; folders and anything that runs are only ever shown in Finder). A screenshot or picture it names (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP) also appears under the reply, like a picture it attached; click it to open it. A path that isn't on this Mac any more is the same chip, struck through and grey ("Not found on this Mac"), and a path anywhere else on your Mac is the same chip with nothing to click.

## Drafts to send

When an agent wants to send an email or a Slack reply as you, you get a draft card. Its title says who and where ("Email to Aurelie · Gmail", "Reply to Aurelie", "Reply in #beta · Slack"; Gmail is named only when the app knows your Gmail account). One block holds To, CC and Subject and the exact words, which scroll if they are long; under it, "From <account> · goes out as you" (just "goes out as you" when the app doesn't know the account) and "These exact words go out under your name." The buttons: **Discard** stops it, **Edit** changes the words first (for an email, also CC and Subject), **Draft in Gmail** opens your own Gmail with the draft filled in and sends nothing, and **Send** is your OK.

A Slack reply posts the words as you edited them. For an email, an edit goes back to your agent, which shows you the new version on a fresh card; what you approve is always exactly what is sent.

## Allow and Deny cards

A request's title says what will happen, in plain words, when the app can tell ("Delete ~/Exports/2024", "Install three@0.170.0", "Run the tests", "Push main to origin"); the raw command is one click away behind **Details**. When it can't tell, the card says "Run a command" and shows the command, as before. When one card covers a whole plan, the button says **Allow all 3** and a line under the list says what that one OK covers ("This one OK covers: send. Each step runs without asking again for an hour."). If the app has a reason for asking, one muted line says it. Once answered, a card folds to one line: its title and what you answered.

## The agent list

Each agent is a row: its key, its name, one line under it and, at the right, when it last moved (`12m`, `1h`, `Mon`) with a small blue dot under the time while there's something unread. A row that needs your OK shows the amber **Needs your OK** tag and nothing else on its line. A working row shows a small grey dot and what it's doing, with how long it's been at it in the time column. Pinned agents carry a small pin and muted ones a bell with a line through it. When the **Catch up** count passes 99 it reads `99+`.

The foot of the sidebar has two rows: **New agent**, then the usage line and a **gear**. The gear's menu holds **Pair your phone**, **Routines** and **All settings**. An empty folder shows a place to **Drag an agent here**; dragging over it says **Drop to move here**.

Drag the sidebar's edge to change its width. Narrower than 240 px the one-line previews go and an amber dot stands in for the tag, "Search" has no shortcut hint and **New agent** keeps its whole name (New folder and New room are in ⌘K and the list's menu). Collapse the sidebar to a rail of keys: a Catch up tile with an amber count, an amber dot on an agent that needs you, a blue dot on unread ones, and the usage icon at the foot. With only the Chief (the first run), a short note, "Your team starts here", and one **New agent** button sit under it.

## Catch up

**Catch up** sits under Search in the Agents sidebar and stays put while the list scrolls: a row the height of a conversation that says how many agents need you and who, or "All clear" (the bell beside the sidebar toggle opens it too, and so does ⌘K). Opening it is a page in the main pane, not a modal: one centred column. The decision waiting first, large, with the thing being approved shown, and **Allow**, **Deny**, **Open chat** or **Later** (Later puts it at the end of **Up next**). Under it, **Up next** lists what else waits, and **While you were away** lists what happened, three rows and then **Show more**. A progress tick row shows where you are. Answering moves to the next; with nothing left you see **All clear**. Keys: A, D, O and L on the card; arrow keys or J and K move through **Up next**; Esc closes.

On your phone, Catch up opens on one card that says how much needs you and from how many agents, with the waiting agents' keys and one button: **Start catching up** (or **Review it** when there's one, or **Continue**, with how many are left and done, when you come back part way). It goes through them one card at a time, with the same Allow, and one last card for what only happened. Under the card, **Or pick one** lists each thing waiting, quietly; tap one to start with it. Offline, the button is off and a line says when the phone last heard from your computer.

## While your agent works

One row at the end of the chat shows your agent's key and one phrase about what it is doing right now, in the app's own words for the step ("Reading Northwind's site", "In Chrome on linkedin.com", "Running the tests · 3 of 8", "Checking with Scout"). Before it has done anything the row says "Working on it". A timer shows after about 8 seconds. After a minute with nothing new it says "Still working" and the time. When it is waiting on you it says "Needs your OK on 12 emails" with a **Review** button that takes you to the card. The three dots appear only while a reply is being written.


## Reply to a message

On the phone, drag any message to the right to reply to it (or hold it and pick Reply). The message follows your finger up to 72 points; past 48 the arrow circle fills and you feel one tap, and letting go sets the reply. It only counts when the drag is mostly sideways, so scrolling is never taken. With VoiceOver or TalkBack, use the Reply action. The reply shows as one outlined line at the top of the message box with a × to cancel; one reply at a time. Once sent, a one-line pill sits above your bubble, outside it, with the words you replied to ("Attachment · name" for a picture, the name first in a room). Tap the pill to go to the original, which glows once; if it's gone the pill says "Message not available". Needs a Mac that supports replies.

Hold any message on the phone for its sheet, always in the same order: **Copy**, **Reply**, **Copy as Markdown** (on a reply with formatting), **Edit and resend**, **Fork from here** and **Rewind to here** (on your own messages), **Remember this**, and **Select text** to copy part of it. A row shows only where that action is possible. Scrolling or swiping a message never opens it.

## Tasks

In the sidebar a row says how much is open ("5 to do", and "14 to do · Busy" with Busy in amber from ten), or "Idle". A working agent keeps its step line until it is Busy, when the row says "14 to do · Busy" instead. On the desk the chat's header is quiet: the agent's key and name, ⌄, and a small dot when there's something to know: amber when something waits on you, red when its last turn didn't finish, blue while it works. Click the name for everything about it right now: who it is and what it runs on; **Now** (what it's doing and for how long, waiting for your OK, didn't finish with **Try again**, or paused until the refill) and **Next turn**, what its next turn runs on, which opens the model choices; its top **Tasks** with **All tasks**; how many requests need your OK with **Review**, which takes you to the first; its **Conversations** (the one on screen ticked, the latest few, **New conversation**); then **Edit agent** and **Instructions and memory**. In a narrow window the same list keeps a search, and renaming a conversation is in ⌘K. On the phone, under an agent's name the muted line is its role while nothing is open; with open work it becomes the task line: a dot, what it is doing, then "4 to do · 1 waits" (the waits in amber when something is waiting on someone), and a chevron. Tap it, or pick Tasks, the first row of the ⋯ menu, to see the list: Doing, Next, Waits on you and Done today (a line the agent dropped stays for the day, muted, with its reason: "Dropped · the lead list was stale"), each line in your own words with who asked and when ("you asked 22:05", "from Mail · 09:10", "since 20:14"). With nothing on the list it says so. The list is read from your Mac; if it can't be reached, the last list stays and says when it was from. On the desk the same list opens from **Tasks**, the first row of the agent's **⋯** menu. That menu has three groups: Tasks, Usage, Routines, Conversations and Files; Instructions and memory, Apps and logins, Model and Independence; then Take over, **Pause** (it becomes **Resume**; a paused agent shows a grey **Paused** tag on its row and in the chat header) and **Delete this agent**. Pausing stops what the agent is doing now and switches its routines off, and Resume turns back on only the routines the pause switched off, never one you had paused yourself. A paused agent still hears you: what you say to it runs. What other agents send it, and what was already waiting in its queue, is kept and runs when you resume it; a routine that was due is skipped. Its row and menu say it is paused. The phone's ⋯ menu has the same row; both need a computer running a recent enough Mogbot. What it was told is at the foot of Instructions and memory, going back is **Rewind to here** on a message, and Pin, Mute, Hide and Share as a recipe are on the agent's row in the sidebar (right-click). Teammates can put a line on an agent's list too; see [Team tasks](asks.md).
