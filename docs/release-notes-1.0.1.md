# Mogbot 1.0.1

## What's new in 1.0.1

- **Onboarding, one step at a time** - setting up the Mac, bringing your agents over and pairing your phone are now one calm screen per decision
- **Remote Access in 5 screens** - pick your assistant, approve once in Tailscale, copy the link; it ticks done by itself
- **What's in flight** - what needs you comes first, with Allow and Deny right there
- **Usage reads as used** - every bar fills as you use it, accounts show their email and plan, and the account new work starts on is marked
- **Polish** - agent keys without the dark outline, one unread dot, agent-coloured loaders, a calmer composer hint, a faster first reply from your Chief of Staff, and the TestFlight link for the iPhone app
